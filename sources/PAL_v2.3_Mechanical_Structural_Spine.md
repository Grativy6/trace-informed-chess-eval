# 1. Keeper and abstract

**PAL is not a mathematical theory and does not seek a final equation. PAL is an ordered mechanical structure for making and carrying distinctions without claiming more than the current layer permits. It begins with unresolved possibility, admits a supplied cut, carries the cut's reflection and trace, records every later cost and witness, and closes only named claims within declared `SCOPE` boundaries. Mathematics may instantiate, test, or strengthen a PAL layer. It does not contain PAL, prove the entire spine, or consume unresolved possibility. Every valid closure retains the conditions under which it can be reopened.**

*PAL is a cumulative list of earned distinctions. Each layer states what has become mechanically available, what had to be supplied to earn it, what must remain carried, what still cannot be claimed, and what would reopen the account.*

## Abstract

PAL v2.3 preserves the complete primitive floor, A1–A15 identities, SC-00–SC-20, four-status closure grammar, and account/receipt lineage of v2.2. It adds typed boundary-use and projection discipline; clarifies structural versus resolution-qualified minimality; routes interface-fiber, return/export, action-lift, checkpoint, cadence, slack, and geometric realizations to PAL v2.3-M; and adds bounded receipt profiles for adapters, checkpoints, heartbeats, and re-entry. These additions create no new primitive, layer, claim status, authority source, physical law, or independent validation. D-FIRST-OCCURRENCE, O04, and O25 remain OPEN.

## Compact operating sentence

Begin unresolved. Admit only witnessed cuts. Carry every surviving difference. Close only the named account. Leave the handle attached.

## Questions PAL Does Not Answer

*Author’s scope statement — Christopher D. Pang*

What’s before the Big Bang? What’s outside the universe? What’s bigger than the multiverse? What is time before time? What is north of the North Pole? What’s more abstract than abstract space? What’s there if you don’t measure it?

As posed, these questions borrow the very frame they ask the receiver to stand outside. They lean on themselves and go nowhere. You need some distance, a supplied separation, for any asymmetry to become closable.

These are the questions PAL does **not** try to answer. Ω is not their hidden answer, and `A_0` is not the first event, cause, or boundary of reality. PAL begins with a supplied cut inside a declared account and asks only what can honestly be distinguished, traced, and carried forward from there.

# 7. Closure and reopening

A15 is a receipt counter, not a finish line. It asks exactly what is being closed, under which `SCOPE`-role `BoundaryRef` and version, with which evidence and tolerance, at whose authority, what remains outside, and what change would reopen the claim.

| **Status**          | **Meaning**                                                                                                    |
|---------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **CLOSED_IN_SCOPE** | The named claim passed its typed checker within the recorded scope; residuals and reopening conditions remain. |
| **OPEN**            | A named obligation or witness is intentionally outstanding and has not been negatively resolved.               |
| **UNRESOLVED**      | Available evidence or rules do not license a clean classification inside the current account.                  |
| **REOPENED**        | A formerly classified or closed claim has a material delta; the former receipt remains in the ledger.          |

Close(q, account) -\> Receipt(q, status, scope, evidence, tolerance, authority, residual, reopen)  
  
Material delta Δ:  
Reopen(q, Δ) -\> REOPENED_current + TRACE_preserved + FORMER_RECEIPT_preserved
