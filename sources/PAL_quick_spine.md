| Address | Mechanical capability | Minimal required witness |
|---|---|---|
| Ω | Unresolved possibility remains nameable but formally unconsumed | No internal witness; A0’s first positive witness must be supplied, never derived from Ω |
| A0 | The first supplied cut admitted within one declared account | Supplied cut witness with account, scope, admission basis, cost, CUT-role BoundaryRef, cut/receipt identity, and provenance |
| Ω* | Cut-indexed reflection kept distinct from A0 without making either side readable | Declared reflection, exchange, complement, involution, or other cut-dependent symmetry witness |
| θ | Trace-bearing turn: orientation changes without yet becoming readable or persistent | Declared turn, action, path, phase change, or orientation transition with predecessor reference |
| A1 | Readable oriented distinction, inheriting orientation from θ | Positive-separation readout, or a sound decoder yielding the same structure |
| A2 | Recoverable consequence and occurrence receipt | Trace store, address policy, protected occurrence, update edge, successful lookup, and receipt identity |
| A3 | Typed retained relation | Admitted relation witness, or protected derivation with resolved endpoint types |
| A4 / SOOP | Identity through budgeted lawful transport | Transport maps, protected observations, comparison law, debt structure, edge bounds, and coherence certificates |
| A5 / SORE | Retained feedback dependence | State, output, readout, update law, comparison domain, output lineage, and nonconstant-dependence certificate |
| A6 / SOSA | Scoped viable or stable organization | Explicitly typed proof of invariance, viability, or stability for an inhabited candidate region |
| A7 | Organization-relative access routing and refresh | Access instance, root and parent receipts, protected queries, route operation, reconstruction rule, and burden account |
| A8 / SIA–SPLAT | Route-caused organizational response | Route-to-effect map, margin, update law, causal-significance rule, thresholds, hysteresis, units, and horizon |
| A9 / χ | Task-relative center of influence | Task observation, continuation domain, equivalence or sufficiency rule, nontrivial work, and valid decoder |
| A10 | Partial comparison among earned centers | Frame identities, partial map or common span, composition domain, comparator, operation order, tie band, and provenance |
| A11 | Coupling and doctrine-relative jointness as coequal typed families | Coupling structure or jointness certificate with its complete declared witness; a combined claim requires both |
| A12 | One-token authority-safe route re-entry | Token identity, checkpoint, consume/emit rule, replay cache, stale-token rule, and mutually exclusive outcomes |
| A13 | Certified cadence | Well-founded rank decreasing on every nonterminal return, or temporal acceptance with explicit fairness assumptions |
| A14 | Cadence-relative quantitative slack | Constraint, ordered codomain and units, CONSTRAINT-role zero, uncertainty interval, evaluator, and predeclared tolerance |
| A15 | Scoped receipt and reopenable closure | Claim, typed checker, proof or invariant, versioned dependencies, scope, authority, tolerance, residual set, and invalidation rule; route closure also requires transition and budget semantics |

A15 returns only CLOSED_IN_SCOPE, OPEN, UNRESOLVED, or REOPENED. Reopening appends history and preserves the former receipt; it never resets the account to Ω.
